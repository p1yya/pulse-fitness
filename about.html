The concept

ABOUT PULSE

PULSE opened in Panchkula with one goal: stop making people choose between the gym that builds their strength and the studio that settles their mind. Most clubs pick a lane — either loud plates and mirrors, or scented candles and silence. We built both, on purpose, under the same roof.

Our vision is a fitness club that follows how people actually train across a real week: a strength circuit on Monday, badminton with colleagues on Wednesday, a slow Vinyasa flow on Friday evening, and ten quiet minutes in the meditation room whenever the day calls for it.

What makes us different

One membership card unlocks the gym, all three courts, the yoga & pilates studio, and the meditation room — no add-on packages.

Built for Panchkula

Sized and scheduled for a real neighbourhood gym, not a chain — small class batches, no equipment queues.

Good to know

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do I need to book gym sessions in advance?

No — the gym floor is walk-in. Yoga, pilates and meditation classes have small batch sizes, so booking a spot is recommended for those.

Is equipment provided for badminton and table tennis?

Rackets and paddles are available to borrow at the front desk at no extra cost; shuttles and balls are provided.

Can I switch membership plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at the front desk at the start of any billing cycle.

Do you offer a trial session?

Yes — reach out through the Contact page and we'll set up a free trial gym session or class.
