Strength & Conditioning

THE GYM FLOOR

Free weights, machines, a dedicated functional zone and a cardio deck overlooking the courts. Certified trainers are on the floor at every session — no guesswork, no queueing for equipment.

01

Free Weight Zone

Dumbbells up to 50kg, Olympic barbells, squat racks and platforms built for deadlifts, done properly.

02

Strength Machines

A full circuit of resistance machines for members easing into structured training, or working around an injury.

03

Cardio Deck

Treadmills, rowers and bikes on the upper deck, with a clear view over the courts below.

04

Functional Zone

Kettlebells, battle ropes, sleds and an open turf strip for conditioning work that doesn't fit on a machine.
