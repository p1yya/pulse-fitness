MEDITATION ROOM

A quiet, low-light room set apart from the rest of the club — no mirrors, no music with a beat, no phones. Guided sessions in the morning, silent sitting in the evening.

Guided & Silent Sessions

20-minute guided breathing sessions run every morning at 6:30. The room is also open for silent, self-led sitting throughout the day for any member who needs ten quiet minutes.
